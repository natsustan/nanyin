# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

macOS

## Users

Nanyin is primarily for its maker and for music listeners who miss classic desktop music software. They value a lightweight, responsive native Mac experience, enjoy historical interface design, and want the interface to stay out of the way while they browse and listen.

## Product Purpose

Nanyin is a native macOS Spotify client focused on making everyday listening faster, lighter, and more visually considered. Success means playback and browsing feel immediate, the app remains dependable during long listening sessions, and optional themes can evoke classic music applications without compromising modern macOS behavior.

## Positioning

The fast, native Mac music client that brings the craft and character of classic desktop players to modern listening.

## Brand Personality

Lightweight, musical, and nostalgic without becoming ornamental. Nanyin should feel like a carefully maintained native Mac application: compact, responsive, familiar, and appreciative of classic interface eras.

## Anti-references

Avoid the oversized cards, loose information density, and generic Electron feel of modern streaming clients. Avoid exaggerated candy-colored Aqua, superficial nostalgia, copied proprietary assets, and period styling that makes the app slower or less usable.

## Design Principles

1. Listening comes first; historical character must support rather than interrupt it.
2. Feel native and immediate through compact information architecture, predictable Mac interactions, and low overhead.
3. Recreate the design logic of an era rather than copying branded pixels.
4. Treat themes as coherent interface systems that may later cover multiple Spotify and iTunes periods.
5. Preserve modern behavior beneath historical appearance, including keyboard access, VoiceOver, clear focus, and system accessibility preferences.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard navigation, VoiceOver semantics, visible focus, Reduce Motion behavior, and modern macOS window conventions across every theme. Historical density may use compact pointer targets, but core actions must remain discoverable and operable without requiring pixel-perfect mouse control.
